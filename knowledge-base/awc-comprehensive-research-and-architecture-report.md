### 1. Executive Summary & Organizational Identity
The **Animal Welfare Coalition (AWC – Sri Lanka)** (ශ්‍රී ලංකා සත්ව සුභසාධන සන්ධානය) operates publicly on Facebook under the handle `@AWCSriLanka` (Page ID 469859479553961, created January 11, 2025). Its mission is:
> *"United for humane solutions to human-animal conflict. Be the voice for the voiceless"*

#### What AWC Is:
* A civil-society animal-welfare **advocacy, legal coordination, policy-reform, and civic mobilization alliance**.
* Coordinates police complaints, instructs lawyers for Magistrate's Court proceedings, accompanies witnesses to police stations, mobilizes regional volunteers for document delivery, lobbies ministries and international bodies (WHO), audits state sterilization budgets, and critiques draft legislation.

#### What AWC Is NOT:
* **NOT** a 24/7 nationwide physical rescue ambulance fleet, veterinary hospital, or shelter.
* Actively campaigns *against* state-run dog shelters/orphanages (which turn into overcrowded disease traps) and champions humane CNVR (Catch-Neuter-Vaccinate-Release) and municipal dog crematoriums.

### 4. Policy Campaigns & Scientific Data
* **One Health Rabies Transfer to DAPH**:
  * Over 90% of bite treatments (>184,000 annually) involve owned domestic pets, not strays.
  * Only 3% of dogs are truly ownerless; 79% of street dogs are unsterilized owned males.
  * Health Ministry spent only 50% of the Rs. 200M 2024 sterilization budget (returned Rs. 100M to Treasury) and only ~Rs. 32M in 2025, leading to AWC's formal Bribery Commission complaint under Section 111 of Anti-Corruption Act No. 9 of 2023.
  * Transfer rabies management to DAPH, which has 700+ veterinarians across 337 Divisional Offices.
* **Opposition to State Shelters**:
  * Facilities in Trincomalee (Rs. 80M), Wennappuwa, and Nellikulama turned into lethal disease centers.
  * AWC proposed municipal canine crematoriums in May 2026 for dignified end-of-life care and state revenue.
* **Critique of 2026 Animal Welfare Bill (Champa Fernando)**:
  * Clause 19 exemptions nullify cruelty protections for agriculture, religious, and cultural practices.
  * Clause 3 places administration under DAPH (conflict of interest with commercial livestock production).
  * Clause 48 restricts animal welfare officers to government vets, excluding wildlife and private companion-animal vets.
  * Clause 13 cosmetic testing loophole.
